9/20/2026 Entry: Creating my own Games

Hello Blog!
This is my first entry, so welcome to the blog! I hope to update you frequently as my games are being created. As this is my first blog, it is only fair to write about my first ever app, which is Sudoku+.
My idea to create a Sudoku game is nothing new, as I’m sure many of you have played Sudoku on your mobile devices before. However, I got the idea to turn Sudoku into a 4-type game due to one of the classic Sudoku books that you can buy at pretty much any airport or convenience store. I got to the end of the book, and there were bonus games of Killer Sudoku. I enjoyed these games so much, and was so sad when the book was completed. I realized that out of all the digital Sudoku games I had played, there had only ever been the normal version or the mini version. That’s when the idea for Sudoku+ really formed in my head: I wanted to make a game that went beyond the classic game, but explored new types of games to give people like me a challenge.
My only issue with that was that I had never created a mobile game before. As a Windows computer user, I quickly learned that you can only make apps ready and available on the iOS store if you built them using Xcode, a software only available on Macbooks. So naturally, I gave up.
That was until a month later, and I realized I still really wanted to build this app. I then thought about it in terms of my long-term goals. I am the type of person to have huge goals that last a week or two and then leave, never to be thought of again. This idea, however, was different. I still had the same energy I had when I first thought of the idea. This is when I made the possibly financially stupid idea to purchase a Macbook Neo. The Neo had just recently come out, and I used this release as a sign from the universe that I needed to make my app dreams a reality. As soon as I got my new, pretty pink computer, I got to work. Within a week, I had the beginnings of my precious Sudoku+ app, and I had never been happier.
I am confident that Sudoku+ will be the first app in a line of many, many more apps. I am truly obsessed with the design process and all the coding that goes into app making. In the future, I plan to make games that are truly unique. Yes, Sudoku+ has its own spin on a classic game, but I have so many more ambitious goal games to build. This is only the beginning of Daisy Chain Apps, and I am so grateful to anyone who is reading my story. I appreciate you so much, and I hope you enjoy following along with me on this journey!
Until next time,
Mars
Developer of Daisy Chain Apps
