Meet the Developer

Hi! I’m Mars Schrag. I’m 20 years old and currently studying Applied Data Analytics at Syracuse University. One thing about me is I have been interested in game design since I was 10 years old. I’ve been part of many coding camps and have coded small computer games in my free time since I learned how. As I’ve gotten older and taken many college level coding courses, I have vastly improved my capabilities. I decided to start learning how to create mobile games this past summer and have decided to put my passion into starting an app studio.

Feel free to follow along with my progress in my blog, which I will be updating as often as I can. I will be talking about my ideas, my wins, my setbacks, and everything in between. If you aren’t the reading type, you can also just jump straight into my apps. Currently, I have created Sudoku+ as my first game, but I will be adding more soon. Thank you so much for your interest in me, and I hope I am able to brighten your day with my games!
